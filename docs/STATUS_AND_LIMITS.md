## 1. What the framework is

A **background-independent, relational, global-spectral** foundational
construction: physical structure emerges from the global spectral properties
of a relational system, not from local dynamics on a predefined spacetime.

## 2. Explicit non-claims (scope boundary)

The framework is **not**, and does **not** claim to be:

- a complete or "100%" Theory of Everything;
- a solved derivation of neutrino mass ordering at the local level;
- a phenomenological model of hadrons, or a replacement for QCD/EFT;
- a framework guaranteeing universal quantum chaos.

There is **no "A4/neutrino-ordering solved" claim** and **no "TOE complete"
claim** anywhere in the public record.

## 3. Validation / falsification history

- **RMT (stands).** The fundamental vacuum and minimal-excitation operators
  show **intermediate (pseudo-integrable) statistics** between Poisson and
  GOE; universal GOE quantum chaos is **not** obtained at the fundamental
  level, and increasing system size/coupling does not generically restore it.
  This *falsifies* a direct hadronic/strongly-chaotic reading and is treated
  as a positive structural constraint. The RMT level-spacing-ratio window
  used as a diagnostic is `r ∈ (0.386, 0.530)` (this `r` is the spacing-ratio
  statistic — **not** any neutrino mass ratio).

## 4. Neutrino mass ordering — layered status

This is the most-scrutinised open question. The honest status has **three
layers**, none of which constitutes a solution:

| Layer | Statement | Status tag |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Local construction | The relevant Majorana scale is a free parameter; the ordering is not derived as a local theorem. | **empirical boundary (open)** |
| Minimal Majorana branch | The minimal / symmetric Majorana-sector choice favors **Normal Ordering**. | **model-selection branch, not forced** |
| Global / swampland argument | A global-spectral (quantum-gravity) consistency argument convergently favors **Normal Ordering**. | **conjectural** (unproven conjectures) |

**Falsification logic.** An inverted-ordering (IO) measurement (e.g. from
JUNO / DUNE / cosmology) would:

- falsify the **minimal Majorana predictive branch**, and
- falsify the **global-spectral Normal-Ordering argument**;
- it would **not** automatically falsify the entire local framework, because
  the local construction admits either ordering (ordering is an empirical
  input there).
