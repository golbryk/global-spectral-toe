namely that the framework "predicts the normal hierarchy" and that an
inverted-hierarchy result "would falsify the theory in its current form."
That statement is **superseded** by the corrected, layered status:

1. **Local derivation is open** — the relevant Majorana scale is a free
   parameter; the ordering is *not* a local theorem (empirical boundary).
2. **Minimal Majorana-sector branch favors Normal Ordering** — but as a
   model-selection branch, *not forced*.
3. **Global-spectral / swampland argument convergently favors Normal
   Ordering** — but *conjecturally* (unproven swampland-type conjectures).

An inverted-ordering measurement would falsify the *predictive (minimal
Majorana) branch* and the *global-spectral Normal-Ordering argument*, **not
automatically the entire local framework**.

See `../docs/STATUS_AND_LIMITS.md` and the Zenodo Version 3 synthesis for the
authoritative wording.
